Industry & product news

OpenAI expands ChatGPT Ads to 31 European markets

OpenAI will begin showing ads to Free and Go tier ChatGPT users across 31 European countries starting August 24, its largest ad expansion to date. Paid Plus, Pro, and Enterprise tiers stay ad-free, and OpenAI says the rollout is built around explicit user consent to align with EU data-protection rules.

OpenAI beefs up safety processes for paying users of its most advanced models

OpenAI said it is stepping up safety and monitoring processes specifically for paid customers using its most capable models, as more users hand the systems complex, sensitive tasks. The move follows the company's recent decision to slow development of its upcoming Astra model over cybersecurity concerns.

Grok 4.6 goes live on Amazon Bedrock

xAI's flagship Grok 4.6 — built for coding, agentic tasks, and long-running work with a 500K context window and configurable reasoning levels — is now generally available on Amazon Bedrock across all supported AWS regions. It's priced at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens.

GLM-5.3 ties Kimi K3 for the top open-weights slot on Artificial Analysis's index

Z.ai's GLM-5.3 scored 60 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, matching Kimi K3 and landing just three points behind Claude Opus 5. Its biggest jump was in agentic evaluation (GDPval-AA v2), where it now trails only Opus 5 — though the weights themselves remain unreleased pending Z.ai's cybersecurity safety review.

Palmyra x6 Technical Report: An Agentic, Tool-Use Model Post-Trained via Anchored Supervised Fine-Tuning

Introduces Palmyra x6, an enterprise-oriented agentic model, and a post-training method ("anchored" SFT) aimed at making tool-use behavior more reliable and less prone to drifting from grounded, verifiable actions.

arxiv.org/abs/2608.16620

Toward Safe LLM Agents: A Survey of Specification, Verification, and Enforcement

A survey of the state of the art in giving LLM agents formal safety guarantees for real-world, potentially irreversible actions. It finds current natural-language-to-formal-spec translation only reaches 24–35% semantic correctness, while runtime monitoring cuts unsafe actions by 40–65% in controlled tests — underscoring how far the field is from robust guarantees.

arxiv.org/abs/2608.14590

KnowSim: Evaluating Information Calibration in LLM Assistants with User Simulators that Learn

Proposes learning user simulators to test whether LLM assistants correctly calibrate what they tell users versus what they withhold or assume, aiming at a more realistic benchmark for conversational information-sharing than static prompts.
