Industry & Products

Nvidia commits up to $105B to finance OpenAI's Ohio data center

Nvidia, OpenAI, and SB Energy are building an 8-gigawatt "PORTS-Pike Technology Campus" in Pike County, Ohio, with Nvidia financing up to $105B of the buildout and putting in $1.5B directly for SB Energy. OpenAI will lease the site for 20 years and exclusively use Nvidia GPUs there — potentially 1.5 million chips, worth an estimated $150-200B in revenue for Nvidia through 2030.

The AI video-generation startup's valuation roughly quadrupled from $1.3B eight months ago. The round was led by DST Global with Goldman Sachs Alternatives, Intel Capital and others participating; Higgsfield says annualized revenue climbed from about $20M a year ago to $700M this August.

Tripwire: statistically certified "safety neurons" for triggering refusal

Zhao, Li, Zhang, and Sun propose identifying specific neurons statistically certified to trigger aligned refusal behavior, aiming for a more reliable and interpretable mechanism for models to reject harmful requests than prompt-level alignment alone.

arXiv:2608.14392

AgentRewind: recoverable execution for long-horizon LLM agents

The paper introduces a runtime framework that checkpoints an agent's context and environment state so it can roll back to an earlier point and resume with knowledge from failed attempts, rather than letting early errors propagate irreversibly through a long task. The authors also release MettleBench, a benchmark of long-horizon engineering assignments, and show improved task success rates over baselines.

arXiv:2608.14380

Reflex: fast, predictive vision-language-action models for reaction-critical manipulation

A new vision-language-action (VLA) model architecture targets robotic manipulation tasks where split-second reactions matter, aiming to close the latency gap between perception and action for real-world robots.
