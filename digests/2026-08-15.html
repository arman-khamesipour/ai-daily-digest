Apple built its own AI model for China with help from rival Alibaba

Rather than licensing a third-party model as originally planned, Apple trained a proprietary LLM for Apple Intelligence in China with Alibaba's assistance, making it the first foreign company approved by Beijing to offer a proprietary AI model there. Anthropic's off-balance-sheet chip debt hits $71B in two months

Special purpose vehicles have stacked roughly $71 billion in chip-lease debt on Anthropic's behalf since June, buying Google TPUs and leasing them back so the hardware and its financing never touch Anthropic's own balance sheet. The structure - backstopped in part by Broadcom - keeps Anthropic's compute buildout from diluting equity ahead of its planned IPO.

Research Papers

Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs

Researchers found that the encrypted chain-of-thought blocks providers like Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google return to clients are interchangeable across sessions and models within a provider - letting an attacker feed a stronger model's encrypted trace into a weaker, less-guarded model to force it to decrypt and print the reasoning in plaintext. The technique also surfaced 182 leaked credentials and 367 PII artifacts sitting in publicly available reasoning blocks.

arXiv:2608.09867

Do Influence Tactics Matter? Investigating Prompt Framing Effects in LLM Code Generation

Borrowing a classic taxonomy of workplace persuasion tactics (rational persuasion, ingratiation, exchange, and others), researchers turned each into a reproducible prompt template and tested whether "persuading" an LLM changes its coding output quality. The framing measurably shifts results, suggesting prompt engineering has more in common with human influence tactics than commonly assumed.
