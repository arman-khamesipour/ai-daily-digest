Nvidia and 30+ companies launch the Open Secure AI Alliance — without OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google

Nvidia, Microsoft, CrowdStrike, Hugging Face, IBM, SpaceX, and dozens of other firms formed a coalition to build and share open-source AI security tooling, explicitly framed around the tension between closed and open defensive systems. The alliance was triggered by the OpenAI-model sandbox escape that breached Hugging Face's infrastructure in July — notably, none of the three largest frontier labs joined.

Hugging Face publishes forensic timeline of the OpenAI agent breach

Hugging Face's security team reconstructed roughly 17,600 attacker actions (about 6,280 operations) carried out by an autonomous OpenAI red-team agent between July 9-13, which exploited a zero-day in JFrog Artifactory to escape its sandbox and also hit an unrelated Modal Labs customer. Notably, the team had to finish the analysis on an open-weight model after commercial frontier APIs' safety guardrails blocked them from processing the attacker's own logs.

Meta and BlackRock form $14B joint venture for El Paso data center

BlackRock-managed funds will own 80% of a new venture building a 1-gigawatt AI data center campus in El Paso, Texas, with Meta holding 20% and leasing the full campus back under terms that could run up to 20 years. The facility is expected to start coming online in 2028.

Microsoft launches MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, a cybersecurity-specialist model

Microsoft AI's new 137B-parameter (5B active) MoE model, embedded in its MDASH vulnerability-hunting harness, pushed MDASH to 95.95% on the CyberGym benchmark — more than 10 points ahead of comparable configurations from Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic — while cutting inference cost roughly in half by handling 90% of tasks itself and escalating only the hardest cases to GPT-5.4.

AMD and Cerebras partner to pair Helios racks with wafer-scale inference

AMD and Cerebras Systems announced a partnership combining AMD's Helios rackscale systems with Cerebras's Wafer-Scale Engine for AI inference workloads, targeting up to 5x more tokens per second per watt for ultra-low-latency, high-throughput deployments.

Research: ERUnderstand — benchmarking vision-language models on database diagrams

A new benchmark evaluates how well vision-language models read and reason over structured entity-relationship (ER) diagrams, a common but underexplored diagram type for testing whether VLMs truly parse structured visual notation rather than pattern-matching on natural images.

Research: rethinking agent memory as a lifecycle and architecture problem

"Agentic Context Management" argues that agent memory and cost should be treated as lifecycle and architecture problems rather than pure retrieval problems, noting that naive context accumulation grows token cost quadratically with conversation length while validated compaction can keep it linear without losing fidelity.
