Anthropic names its first Chief Global Affairs Officer

Anthropic hired Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar, a former California Supreme Court justice and past president of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, to lead policy, government relations, and international engagement. He'll report to President Daniela Amodei as the company navigates escalating tensions with the Trump administration over AI regulation.

White House to meet OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and other labs on a voluntary safety framework

The Trump administration is convening the major AI developers to discuss a new voluntary safety-testing framework, giving the government early access to frontier models before public release. The meeting follows weeks of fallout from OpenAI's Hugging Face agent breach, and critics note the same labs are being asked to help write the rules meant to govern them.

Research Papers

Why Large Language Models Fail at Tabular Prediction

Testing a frontier LLM in pure inference mode (no tools, no fine-tuning) against 252 classical models on tabular prediction, researchers find it behaves like a local distance-based classifier in low dimensions but diverges from every classical baseline as dimensionality grows — pinpointing why LLMs still lag purpose-built tabular models.

arxiv.org/abs/2608.02412

SWE-Touch: Benchmarking Coding Agents When Users Touch the Code

Most coding-agent benchmarks assume the agent works alone; SWE-Touch instead injects plausible, conflicting user edits mid-task to see how agents cope when a human is actively modifying the same codebase. Nine coding models were evaluated on SWE-bench Verified plus longer-horizon tasks from SWE-Bench Pro and DeepSWE.

arxiv.org/abs/2608.02499

V-Mem: Modality-Routed Retrieval for Long-Term Multimodal Agentic Memory

Agent memory systems built around text tend to fail on vision-related follow-up questions because queries retrieve same-modality content even in joint embedding spaces. V-Mem routes retrieval by modality and uses an LLM-generated "anchor" to close the gap, outperforming prior multimodal memory baselines on the Mem-Gallery and LoCoMo benchmarks.
