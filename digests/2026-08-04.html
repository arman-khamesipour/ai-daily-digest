Paper: agent-safety benchmarks may be measuring capability, not safety

A validity audit of four widely used agent-safety benchmarks (R-Judge, InjecAgent, AgentHarm, AgentDojo) across 22 models finds their scores often track general model capability rather than genuine safety behavior — on R-Judge, an "always positive" policy beats five of 21 real models on F1, and the benchmarks disagree sharply on how they rank the same 18 models.

Paper: the best AI models still lean toward guessing "stranger"

FriendBench tests whether models can tell, from a short clip of two people talking, whether they already know each other. Top models match human accuracy overall, but get there differently — humans guess "friends" and "strangers" in balanced proportion, while models systematically lean toward guessing "stranger."
