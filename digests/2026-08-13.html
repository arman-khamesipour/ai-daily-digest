Industry & Product News

Google's Gemini app crosses 1 billion monthly users

Sundar Pichai announced the Gemini app has passed 1 billion monthly active users, matching ChatGPT's milestone from June and making it the 14th Google product to reach that scale. Google didn't disclose paying-subscriber counts, and the figure excludes Gemini usage embedded in Search, Workspace, and Android.

Anthropic to watermark all Claude-generated text and images

Anthropic said models released after August 2 will automatically embed invisible watermarks in generated text and files, designed to survive copy-paste so platforms can verify AI origin. The move is driven by the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency mandate but will apply to Claude users worldwide.

Anthropic courts investors ahead of a September/October IPO

The Wall Street Journal reports Anthropic is meeting potential investors to shore up confidence and address public backlash over AI, aiming to go public before OpenAI. Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan are lead underwriters; Anthropic was valued at $965B in its May Series H.

Lovable raises $400M at a $13.3B valuation

The Stockholm-based "vibe coding" startup closed a Series C led by Menlo Ventures and the EU's Scaleup Europe Fund, months after hitting $500M in annualized run-rate revenue. It's now one of Europe's most highly valued private tech companies.

TechCrunch

LiteLLM supply-chain breach reportedly hit 2,500+ companies

Threat-intel firm CloudSEK says a March compromise of two LiteLLM PyPI package versions - entered via a poisoned Trivy scanner in LiteLLM's build pipeline - may have exposed roughly 434,000 CI/CD pipelines. High-confidence affected organizations reportedly include Nvidia, AWS, Cisco, and Salesforce.

REDAgentBench: Executable Red Teaming and Faithful Measurement of LLM Agent Systems

A benchmark of 1,661 executable attack cases across 15 intervention strategies and 5 service surfaces finds a 65.69% average attack success rate across six models. Notably, nearly one in five confirmed violations happens even after the agent explicitly states the relevant safety constraint - a "Recognition-Execution Gap" that a training-free policy reminder cuts by over 70 points.

SHE: Trajectory-driven Safety Harness Evolution for LLM Agents

Researchers from Shanghai AI Lab, Fudan, SJTU, and HKUST argue agent safety depends as much on the harness (system prompt, rule bank, memory, tool policy) as on model weights. Their SHE framework converts rollout failures into structured diagnoses and evolves each harness component independently to close safety gaps.
