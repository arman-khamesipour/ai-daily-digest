"Grading the Graders": a proposed standard for LLM verifier claims

The paper argues the term "verification level" is used inconsistently across the LLM-reasoning literature and proposes Verification Autonomy Levels (VAL, L0–L5), a single axis classifying schemes by where their verification spec comes from and what the verdict actually guarantees.

A broad review of how backdoor/trojan attacks are inserted into LLMs and VLMs (via data poisoning, fine-tuning, or parameter manipulation) and the current landscape of defenses, aimed at consolidating a fast-growing and fragmented research area.
