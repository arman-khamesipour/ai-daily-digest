Industry & Products

Demis Hassabis steps down as Google DeepMind CEO

Hassabis is handing day-to-day operations to CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu, who becomes SVP reporting directly to Sundar Pichai, while Hassabis moves into a chairman and Alphabet chief scientist role to focus on long-term strategy and Isomorphic Labs. The reshuffle follows Jeff Dean's departure to launch his own AI research startup, deepening the sense of upheaval atop Google's AI effort just as it races OpenAI and Anthropic.

Fortune

OpenAI makes GPT-5.6 Luna the default ChatGPT model

OpenAI is rolling Luna out as the default model for all free and paid ChatGPT users this week, saying factual errors are 62% less common than with the previous default, GPT-5.5. The move follows Luna's price cuts earlier this month and pushes OpenAI's newest model to its full user base rather than early access tiers.

Axios

Liquid AI ships a phone-ready on-device agent model

LFM2.5-2.6B is a 2.6B-parameter model trained for agentic workloads — planning, tool calls, multi-step tasks — that runs in under 2.5GB of memory and hits roughly 220 tokens/sec on an Apple M5 Max, with usable speeds on phones. Liquid AI says it beats Qwen3.5-9B on tool-use and instruction-following benchmarks despite being a fraction of the size.

Sierra launches Context Engine for long-running enterprise agents

The product turns a business's accumulated customer data into decisions Sierra's "Horizon" agents can act on across days or weeks, not just single conversations, with each interaction meant to make the next one smarter. It's Sierra's bet that proprietary business context, not raw model capability, is becoming the real moat in enterprise AI deployments.

Research

The crisis of AI-generated mathematics

A new preprint argues for "total opposition" to AI use in mathematics research, responding to recent cases of AI systems autonomously proving theorems and drafting papers. The author lays out proposals for how mathematicians, departments, journals, and institutions could respond to protect the field's norms.

arXiv:2608.02859

S³: Improving Agent Safety through Multi-Stage Defense

The paper observes that safety risks in LLM agent workflows emerge at different stages — memory, planning, tool execution — and can propagate across steps undetected. It introduces reusable "Stage-Specific Safety Skills" plus a pipeline to convert existing safety designs into them, reporting better safety and utility trade-offs than prior baselines.

arXiv:2608.02683

Diagnosing Tool-Selection Reasoning in LLM Agents with Canary Tools

Standard agent evals tell you a model picked the wrong tool but rarely why. This work plants diagnostic "canary tools" into an agent's MCP tool set, each engineered to probe a specific tool-selection failure mode, to localize the reasoning breakdown rather than just scoring the outcome.
