Google DeepMind launches Gemini Robotics 2 for whole-body humanoid control

DeepMind shipped a new suite of three robotics models — a vision-language-action model, an embodied-reasoning model, and an on-device controller — that let humanoids walk, crouch, and manipulate objects together, not just move their arms. In testing it hit a 92% success rate on a full-body task like unscrewing a light bulb, and DeepMind paired the release with a new "ASIMOV-Agentic" safety benchmark for refusing dangerous commands.

Microsoft's AI stakes swing its Q4 earnings: $3.2B Anthropic gain, $600M OpenAI writedown

Microsoft's Q4 FY26 results showed Azure crossing $100B in annual revenue for the first time, with a $3.2B gain from its Anthropic investment adding 33 cents to EPS, offset by a $600M writedown on its OpenAI stake. CEO Satya Nadella continued pitching Microsoft's own MAI models and Copilot as a cheaper alternative to the two labs it's invested billions in.

Nscale buys Anyscale for $1.65B to move up the AI compute stack

Data-center and GPU-cloud operator Nscale is acquiring Anyscale, the company behind the open-source Ray distributed-computing framework, in a bid to own the software layer that decides how efficiently GPUs get used, not just the power and racks underneath them.

Qualcomm completes acquisition of AI compiler startup Modular

Qualcomm closed its all-stock acquisition of Modular, the maker of the Mojo language and MAX inference platform, folding its AI-native software infrastructure into Qualcomm's compute hardware business. Modular's open ecosystem — Mojo, MAX, and Modular Cloud — will continue under its own brand.

EU opens €10B tender for seven AI "gigafactories"

The European Commission formally launched its call for tenders to build seven AI gigafactories across the bloc, backed by €10B in public funding and aiming to unlock over €30B including private investment. 77 proposals across 16 member states were already submitted informally; AMD, Nvidia, and Qualcomm have signed letters of intent to supply chips.

FCC bans Chinese-made humanoid and quadruped robots over confirmed backdoors

Citing documented backdoors in Unitree hardware already deployed at MIT, Princeton, and Carnegie Mellon — including a wormable Bluetooth exploit granting root access — the FCC added foreign-made humanoid and quadruped robots to its Covered List, barring new models from import authorization. China holds roughly 85% of the affected market.

Critical "RufRoot" flaw lets attackers hijack Ruflo AI agent deployments

Security researchers disclosed CVE-2026-59726, a maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) flaw in Ruflo, a popular open-source AI agent orchestration platform. An unauthenticated HTTP request to its MCP bridge could run arbitrary shell commands, steal provider API keys, and poison agent memory; a fix shipped in version 3.16.3.

StabilityBench: benchmarking how LLMs wobble under realistic multi-turn pressure

A new benchmark turns static single-turn evals into multi-turn conversations that inject realistic user behavior — demographic proxies, sycophantic baits — while preserving the original task. Testing nine LLMs on augmented AIME, GSM8k, HealthBench, and StrongReject shows meaningful instability that single-turn scores miss.

Who audits the agent fleet? A budget-allocation approach for overseeing many LLM agents

With one human overseeing many LLM agents, this paper studies how to allocate a limited audit budget when the agents' own confidence signals are miscalibrated and correlated with each other — a realistic setting for agent-fleet deployments where blindly trusting self-reported confidence risks missing correlated failures.
