Nvidia weighs $250B backstop for OpenAI's Ohio data center

Nvidia is reportedly in talks to guarantee up to $250 billion in financing for OpenAI's leased capacity at a planned 10-gigawatt, $500B+ data center campus in Piketon, Ohio — needed in part because still-unprofitable OpenAI can't get an investment-grade credit rating on its own. A separate, similarly sized Nvidia commitment could help fund the chip purchases themselves, deepening scrutiny over circular AI financing.

Nvidia, AMD, ASML, and Micron all sold off after reports that a Shanghai-based, state-backed firm has begun mass-producing homegrown deep-UV lithography machines — a category ASML has dominated for years — raising fears that China is closing the gap on leading-edge chipmaking tools.

Celeris-1: a diffusion-based LLM built for speed

New AI lab Celeris launched Celeris-1, a general-purpose language model that swaps standard autoregressive generation for a diffusion-based architecture, delivering a 157ms median response time — roughly 15-17x faster than GPT-5/GPT-5-mini — while scoring competitively on MMLU-Pro (76% vs. 78-81%).
