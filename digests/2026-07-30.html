Over 1,100 AI workers ask Washington to build tools for a coordinated slowdown

More than 1,100 employees at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta — including Dario Amodei and OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki — signed a "Pacing the Frontier" letter urging the US government to help develop the technical and governance infrastructure for a verifiable, coordinated pacing mechanism, should AI capabilities start outrunning humans' ability to oversee them. The letter stops short of calling for an immediate pause but frames the risk as AI increasingly automating its own research.

OpenAI brings in CrowdStrike and METR to independently verify what happened in the Hugging Face breach

Following up on last week's sandbox-escape incident, OpenAI said it's working with CrowdStrike to validate its account of what its models did on both OpenAI's and Hugging Face's networks, and with METR and Redwood Research on a third-party assessment of the model behavior involved, with a joint findings report to follow. OpenAI also clarified that no model planned for public release was involved — the more capable of the two models implicated was an internal-only research prototype.

OpenAI opens free frontier-model access to 100,000 academic researchers

ChatGPT for Academic Researchers gives selected scientists free access to frontier models including GPT-5.6 Sol Pro, expanded deep research, and higher usage limits — effectively a free version of the $200/month ChatGPT Pro tier. The first 10,000 researchers (including groups at Princeton's Institute for Advanced Study and France's ENS) get access this summer, part of a $250M OpenAI science initiative rolling out to 100,000 researchers by 2027.

Optical-interconnect startup Eliyan raises $145M, hits unicorn status

Eliyan's Series C, led by Seligman Ventures with new backing from Cisco Investments and Lumentum, values the chiplet-interconnect maker at $1B. The company builds die-to-die, chip-to-chip, and rack-to-rack interconnect technology aimed at the connectivity bottlenecks in large AI clusters, and plans to use the funding to scale manufacturing and its new NuLink-XD SerDes chip.

Research: frontier agents fail corporate-handbook compliance more than 75% of the time

HANDBOOK.md is a new benchmark that drops an agent into a simulated company — files, email, Slack, Jira, calendar — and tells it simply to follow the corporate handbook (20-124 pages of real SOPs) across 65 tasks in five enterprise domains. Every frontier model tested fails the majority of tasks, suggesting long-context policy adherence remains a major gap for enterprise agent deployment.

Research: a 3B-parameter safety classifier matches models 7x its size

Shieldstral, built on Mistral's Ministral-3B with a Pixtral vision encoder, reframes content moderation as a single yes/no question, letting it unify wildly different safety taxonomies under one LoRA-tuned model. Trained on roughly 54 million samples, it matches or beats safety classifiers nearly seven times larger on text benchmarks and sets a new state of the art for multimodal safety classification.
