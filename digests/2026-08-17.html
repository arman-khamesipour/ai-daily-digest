Stripe reportedly closes $7B+ acquisition of AI gateway OpenRouter

Stripe has finalized a deal to acquire OpenRouter, the startup that lets developers route requests across 400+ AI models from a single API, for more than $7 billion — over 5x the $1.3B valuation it carried just three months ago. The deal gives Stripe a stronger foothold in AI infrastructure, positioning it as a neutral switchboard for companies choosing between models on cost and performance.

Alibaba's Qwen models pass 3 billion downloads, overtaking Meta and Google

Alibaba's open-weight Qwen family has topped 3 billion cumulative downloads in the past six months, according to a Hugging Face state-of-open-models report, eclipsing Google (418M) and Meta (227M) to become the world's most-downloaded AI model line. Qwen has open-sourced 460+ models and spawned over 300,000 community derivatives, cementing it as a default base for developers fine-tuning and deploying open models.

Paper: Synthetic Persona Pretraining aligns models "from token zero"

Researchers propose Synthetic Persona Pretraining (SPP), which installs an aligned assistant persona from the very start of pretraining by annotating training documents with value-aligned first-person reflections, rather than bolting on alignment during post-training. In 3B-parameter models trained on 500B tokens, the technique cut jailbreak attack success rates by 63% and improved constitution-following versus applying the same reflections only late in pretraining.

Paper: LittleLearner probes what LLMs know when trained only on a grade-school curriculum

The paper introduces LittleCurriculum, an 88B-token corpus built from U.S. elementary-school material, and trains a 5B-parameter model called LittleLearner on it exclusively. By tightly controlling knowledge exposure to a known curriculum, the authors get a testbed for studying what a language model actually learns versus memorizes, with clean, mappable knowledge and capability boundaries.
