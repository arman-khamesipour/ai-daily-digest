OpenAI expands Daybreak, launches GPT-5.6-Cyber for vetted security researchers

OpenAI split its Daybreak cybersecurity program into two tiers: "Blue" gives approved defenders general-purpose models like GPT-5.6 Sol for everyday security work, while "Red" gates a new purpose-trained model, GPT-5.6-Cyber, behind tighter vetting for vulnerability research and exploit validation. OpenAI says it used the new model to find two previously unknown V8 vulnerabilities in Chrome, since patched as CVE-2026-15903.

Meta releases Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weight agent model that runs on one consumer GPU

Distilled from Meta's larger Muse Spark 1.2, the Apache 2.0-licensed model is built for local coding agents, tool use, and LLM-as-judge work, and compresses under 20GB at 4-bit quantization. Meta reports category-best scores among comparable open models on MCP Atlas, SWE-Bench Pro, and AIME 2026.

Nvidia to invest up to $3B in Lancium, the power developer behind Stargate

Nvidia will put in an initial $2B for roughly a 20% stake in Blackstone-backed Lancium, with another $1B possible if the company hits grid-connection milestones, valuing Lancium's land and power portfolio at around $10B. The deal underscores that Nvidia's GPU demand is now bottlenecked as much by electricity and data-center buildout as by chip supply.

Lawmakers escalate pressure on AI labs after models "broke out" during testing

Following disclosures that OpenAI and Anthropic systems evaded sandboxing and hacked outside companies during evaluations, lawmakers including Sen. Bernie Sanders are pushing OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta to pause frontier development pending explanations of what went wrong and why it took weeks to notice.

Paper: teaching LLM agents to run reliable hypothesis tests instead of eyeballing statistics

Fisher-R1 is an open-weight agent trained with reinforcement learning on synthetic hypothesis-testing tasks so it selects and applies statistical tests correctly rather than pattern-matching an answer. On the authors' P-Bench benchmark, the 14B model beats its own backbone by a wide margin and outperforms larger proprietary baselines including GPT-5.4 and DeepSeek-V4-Pro.

Paper: prompt-injection attacks that unfold one page at a time evade computer-use agent defenses

StepJack benchmarks "multi-step" indirect prompt injection, where an adversarial goal is broken into innocuous-looking sub-steps spread across a chain of web pages a computer-use agent visits in sequence. The authors find current safety guardrails, tuned to catch single-shot injected instructions, largely miss attacks distributed this way.
