OpenAI's revenue run rate tops $40B ahead of a potential IPO

OpenAI is on pace for more than $40B in annualized revenue, roughly double its end-of-2025 run rate, driven by coding tools, subscriptions, and a nascent ads business. The company has filed confidentially with the SEC, though a $7B employee tender offer this week suggests the public listing may not be imminent.

OpenAI hires Wiz alum as new Chief Revenue Officer

Greg Brockman announced Dali Rajic, previously of Wiz (acquired by Google for $32B), as OpenAI's new CRO, the latest move in an ongoing executive shake-up at the company as it scales its enterprise and revenue operations ahead of a possible public offering.

Cognition, maker of coding agent Devin, in talks to raise at a $40B valuation

Three months after raising $1B at a $26B valuation, Cognition is reportedly in early talks for a new round that could value it above $40B, with annualized revenue nearing $1B - roughly double what it reported in May. Customers include Goldman Sachs and Mercedes-Benz.

Bloomberg

Manus to resume as an independent company as Meta unwinds its $2B acquisition

China's NDRC ordered Meta to withdraw its December 2025 acquisition of the AI agent startup, citing tech-export and foreign-investment rules, and Meta has since cut Manus staff off from its internal systems. Manus says it will soon operate independently again, with some users asked to back up data generated since the deal was announced.

Built into Microsoft's MDASH harness, the model reportedly outperforms Anthropic's Mythos 5, Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, and OpenAI's GPT-5.5 Cyber on the CyberGym benchmark at roughly half the cost, as Microsoft leans into AI-vs-AI dynamics in cybersecurity.

Microsoft bought 8.55 million metric tons of carbon-removal credits in the year through mid-July - about a fifth of what it purchased over the same period in 2025 - as capital increasingly shifts toward AI infrastructure buildout even as the company maintains its 2030 carbon-negative pledge.

InfraBench: Evaluating Infrastructure Agents Across Layers, Lifecycle, and Risk

A new benchmark suite tests AI agents on realistic infrastructure-management tasks across the full system stack and operational lifecycle, with fine-grained risk scoring. Across 15 agent-model configurations, even the strongest agent can't secure a full score, and repeating tasks three times shows top configurations still pass only a fraction of their attempts.

arXiv:2608.11234

Benchmarking LLM Judges for Mobile Agent Evaluation

MobileJudgeBench tests six LLM-as-judge methods against 931 human-annotated mobile-agent trajectories spanning 68 apps. A simple baseline judge using sampled screenshots matches or beats purpose-built judging pipelines, suggesting judge quality is driven more by the underlying LLM than by pipeline sophistication.

arXiv:2608.11434

FrontierFinance: A Challenging Benchmark for Measuring Frontier Intelligence of Finance Agents

Existing finance-agent benchmarks mostly test narrow data extraction that current models have already saturated. FrontierFinance instead offers 220 expert-crafted queries and 11,543 source-attributed rubrics spanning the full investor-research workflow, aiming to better separate models on open-ended analyst-style tasks.
